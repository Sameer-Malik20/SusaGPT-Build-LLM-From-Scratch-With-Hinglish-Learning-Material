# AI Foundations and Operations (MLOps)

Ye folder humare AI curriculum ka core hai. Isme mathematical foundations, machine learning principles, aur AI models ko production me scale par deploy karne ke liye required operational excellence cover kiya gaya hai.

## 🧠 What You Will Learn (Aap Kya Seekhenge)

- **Mathematical Foundations**: AI ke liye Linear Algebra, Calculus, aur Probability.
- **Python for AI**: AI development ke liye customized software engineering best practices.
- **ML & Deep Learning**: Classical algorithms se lekar deep neural networks tak.
- **Transformer Architecture**: Modern LLMs ke peeche ka 'Engine'.
- **LLM Training & Tuning**: Pre-training, fine-tuning, aur performance optimization.
- **Vector Databases**: RAG aur search systems ke liye efficient retrieval.
- **MLOps & LLMOps**: AI systems ka continuous integration, delivery, aur monitoring.
- **AI Infrastructure**: GPUs (H100/A100), networking, aur hardware ko samajhna.
- **Production AI**: Scaling, deployment strategies, aur observability.

## 💼 Career Roles (Career options)

Is folder ko complete karne ke baad, aap in roles ke liye prepare kar sakte hain:
- **MLOps Engineer**
- **AI Infrastructure Engineer**
- **Machine Learning Engineer**
- **AI Research Engineer**
- **LLM Operations Specialist**
